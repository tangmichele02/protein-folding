What am I looking at?

The ribbon traces the protein's backbone: the chain of amino acids (residues) as AlphaFold predicts it folds. Coils are α-helices, flat arrows are β-strands. Color is pLDDT, AlphaFold's confidence in each residue's local shape. Orange stretches are often genuinely floppy ("intrinsically disordered") regions, not just bad predictions.

What am I looking at?

Cell (x, y) is AlphaFold's expected error, in ångströms (1 Å = 0.1 nm, about one atom's radius), in the position of residue x if the prediction is superimposed on the true structure at residue y. Dark means the two residues' relative placement is trustworthy. Dark squares along the diagonal are domains, independently folded units. Light off-diagonal blocks mean the domains themselves are confident but how they're arranged relative to each other is not.
